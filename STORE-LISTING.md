## Short description

## Full description

Find your flow with Cue, a simple teleprompter for rehearsing presentations, practising speeches, and reading scripts while filming with a separate camera.

WRITE AND ORGANISE

Write a new script or paste text you already have. Search your library, keep favourite scripts close, and see an estimated reading time before you begin.

READ AT YOUR PACE

Start with an optional three-second countdown. Adjust the scrolling speed and text size, pause whenever you need, or swipe back to find your place. A reading guide helps you stay on track.

MIRROR YOUR SCRIPT

Turn on horizontal mirror mode when using teleprompter glass. Use Cue in portrait or landscape, depending on your setup.

KEEP IT SIMPLE

Cue works offline and stores scripts on your device. No account is required. This version contains no ads or in-app purchases.

Cue displays scripts; it does not record video, follow your voice, or float above other apps. Use a separate camera or another device to record. Reading speed is an estimate. Scripts are stored locally and are removed if you uninstall the app or clear its data.
